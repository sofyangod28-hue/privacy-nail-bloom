NAIL BLOOM · READY COM

Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 13 October 2026
Android application: com.nailbloom.nailnail

READY COM develops Nail Bloom, an app for nail-art lessons, drawing, videos, quizzes, and certificates. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by the app and its advertising services.

For questions, support, or privacy requests, contact kolonil97@outlook.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Nail Bloom does not require an account. Lesson progress, toolkit checklists, quiz answers and results, saved nail designs, and certificate details are stored locally in the app’s storage on your device.

If you enter a learner name for a certificate, that name is stored locally and included in the certificate you export. READY COM does not operate a server to receive or synchronize these items, and the app does not intentionally send them to advertising partners.

Lessons and bundled tutorial videos can be used offline. The app may copy video assets into its local cache for playback. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise device location.

2. Files you export

You can export a design or certificate as an image using Android’s file picker. You choose where the file is saved. If you select a cloud-storage provider or share an exported file with another app, that service handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Exported copies may remain outside Nail Bloom after you clear its storage or uninstall it.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Nail Bloom uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) for banner and interstitial advertising. Its advertising integration includes Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, and Unity Ads. Which partner serves an ad depends on availability and the advertising configuration.

Advertising services may process advertising or other app and device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and operating-system details, app version, network information, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic or performance data. Advertising SDKs may also store information on your device.

These services use information to deliver ads, personalize ads where permitted, measure advertising performance, diagnose problems, and prevent fraud or abuse. Advertising and mediation providers receive and process information through their SDKs. Their own policies describe further processing, sharing, retention, and privacy choices.

  • Google: Privacy Policy and Advertising information .
  • Unity, including LevelPlay/ironSource and Unity Ads: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy .
  • Meta Audience Network: Meta Privacy Policy .

4. Consent and advertising choices

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent when required and when a message is configured for your region.

Where available, use the app’s Ad privacy choices option to review or change your choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and advertising privacy options in your Android device settings.

Available settings depend on your Android version and region. Limiting personalization does not necessarily remove ads or all processing needed to deliver, measure, or protect advertising.

5. Contacting READY COM

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, provide support, and handle privacy requests.

Email is processed through our email service provider, Microsoft Outlook. Please send only information needed for your request.

6. Retention, deletion, and security

Local app information remains until you remove it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Saved designs can be deleted within the app. Clearing app storage removes local progress and certificate information.

You must delete separately any files you exported to other locations.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to handle the request and relevant legal obligations. Advertising partners determine retention for information they process, as described in their policies.

We take reasonable care with information under our control, but no device, transmission, or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and international processing

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority.

Contact us at kolonil97@outlook.com for information under READY COM’s control. For information controlled by advertising providers, use their privacy contacts and controls. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, consent is used for advertising activities that require it. Support requests and service security are handled on the applicable service-related or legitimate-interest basis. Providers explain their own legal bases and safeguards in their policies.

Email and advertising services may process information outside your country, subject to their stated safeguards and applicable law.

8. Children’s privacy

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to READY COM should contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps.

Avoid including a child’s full name or other identifying details in support messages unless necessary. Advertising providers’ policies describe their handling of children’s information and applicable privacy protections.

9. This policy page and future changes

This page includes no analytics scripts, advertising, or forms. The hosting service may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and server logs, to deliver and secure the page.

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its date, and provide additional notice when required.
